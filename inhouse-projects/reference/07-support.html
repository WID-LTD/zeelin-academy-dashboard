Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit my project deliverables? +
Navigate to the Required Deliverables section in your project sidebar. You can drag and drop files or click the upload zone to submit artifacts.
What happens if I fail the milestone quiz? +
You get one additional attempt. Review your mentor feedback and project brief before retrying.
Can I change my assigned mentor? +
Contact the Support Team via Community Chat to request a mentor reassignment.
How does the accountability tracking work? +
Your progress, peer reviews, and mentor evaluations are tracked in real-time on your Accountability dashboard.
