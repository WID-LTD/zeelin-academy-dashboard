Retail Enterprise Inventory & Supply Chain Optimization

Welcome to your Stage 2 In-House Project. This practical assignment simulates real-world business analysis consulting. You are tasked with analyzing a failing retail supply chain network, eliciting operational requirements from stakeholders, and modeling a comprehensive inventory management solution.

Core Project Objectives

  • Stakeholder Mapping: Identify key supply chain actors, warehouse managers, and retail vendors.
  • Requirements Traceability: Document functional and non-functional requirements in the provided template.
  • Data Modeling: Build inventory turnover and cost-benefit projections using spreadsheet tools.
